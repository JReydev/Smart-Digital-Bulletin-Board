Install SmartBulletin on iOS

Follow these simple steps to install SmartBulletin as a native app on your iPhone or iPad

1

Open Safari Browser

Make sure you're using Safari browser on your iOS device. The app cannot be installed from other browsers like Chrome or Firefox.

  • Go to your device's home screen
  • Tap on the Safari icon
  • Navigate to your SmartBulletin website
2

Tap the Share Button

Look for the share button at the bottom of Safari (it looks like a square with an arrow pointing up).

  • Scroll to the bottom of the page if needed
  • Tap the share icon (📤)
  • This will open the iOS share menu
3

Select "Add to Home Screen"

In the share menu, scroll down and look for "Add to Home Screen" option.

  • Scroll through the share options
  • Find and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  • You might need to scroll down to see it
4

Customize the App Name

You can customize the app name or keep the default "SmartBulletin".

  • The app name will be pre-filled
  • You can edit it if you want
  • Tap "Add" when ready
5

App Installed Successfully!

The SmartBulletin app will now appear on your home screen with the SmartBulletin icon.

  • You can move it around like any other app
  • Tap it to open the app
  • It will open in full-screen mode like a native app
💡 Pro Tip: After installation, when you open the app, you'll be automatically redirected to the login page for security. This ensures that each user needs to log in with their own credentials.

Troubleshooting

If you're having trouble installing the app:

  • Make sure you're using Safari: Other browsers don't support PWA installation on iOS
  • Check iOS version: You need iOS 11.3 or later
  • Clear Safari cache: Go to Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data
  • Restart Safari: Close and reopen Safari, then try again
  • Check website URL: Make sure you're on the correct SmartBulletin website
